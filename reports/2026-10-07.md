# New SLC commercial permits and planning applications: Sep 23 – Oct 07, 2026

**12 new record(s).**

**Highlights**

- **26TMP-022171** – 100 S West Temple St: A commercial demolition application was filed for this downtown address. The record has no description, so the scope and the building involved are not yet known.
- **26TMP-022279 / 26TMP-022245** – Salt Lake City International Airport (3920 W 900 N): Two airport retail renovations, of 605 sf and 881 sf, carry project names pairing King's English Bookshop with KSL and with Trip Advisor.
- **BLD2026-08226** – 1070 S 3800 W, Suite 700: Medical-device company Penumbra is reconfiguring 19,000 sq ft of warehouse racking. The work is valued at $221,462.

**Planning pipeline**

One Planning record came in: a preliminary subdivision application (26TMP-022261) at 1436 S El Rey St on the east side. The number of lots is not stated.

**Themes**

- **Airport concessions:** Four of the 12 records are small renovations of existing airport-terminal retail stores, each 590 to 881 sf, filed the same day. They cover SLC Essentials (post-security and Concourse B) and two King's English Bookshop co-branded shops.
- **Mostly routine:** The rest of the batch is trade work. That includes a sign-lighting electrical permit in the Capitol Hill Historic District, a three-phase service change, building-automation controls, a fire-sprinkler filing for "Ville 1990" on North Temple, and a city test record.
