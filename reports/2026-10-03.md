# New SLC commercial permits and planning applications: Sep 19 – Oct 03, 2026

**10 new record(s).**

**Highlights**

- **PLNAPP2026-00854** (707 W Genesee Ave): Appeal of the Planning Commission's 09/22/2026 design-review decision for the West End Apartments at approximately 730 W 900 S (PLNPCM2024-01450). The appeal argues the decision violates the religious freedom of the adjacent house of worship, Summum, and the city's design review standards.
- **PLNPCM2026-00852** (16 W 800 S): Master plan amendment application titled "IHC Skybridge" goes to the Planning Commission.

**Planning pipeline**

Two Planning Commission-level items were filed: an appeal over the West End Apartments design review, and a master plan amendment for an "IHC Skybridge" near 800 S. A placeholder lot consolidation was also filed at 2591 S 800 E.

**Themes**

The building permits are mostly small trade and tenant work. The largest is a $150,000 equipment enclosure for Stadler Rail's CNC Hall at 5880 W 150 S. Other work includes building-automation upgrades on the west side and a unit reconfiguration in the University Historic District.
