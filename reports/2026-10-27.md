# New SLC commercial permits and planning applications: Oct 13 – Oct 27, 2026

**16 new record(s).**

**Highlights**

- **Power HRG tenant improvement, $3.7M** (BLD2026-08823, 6228 W 700 S): A remodel of an existing 38,085 sq ft, one-story building into office, assembly and warehouse space. Method Studio filed it.
- **Chick-fil-A Sugar House drive-thru expansion, $1.37M** (BLD2026-08835, 1206 E 2100 S): The work expands the drive-thru lane and adds new order canopies, a kitchen equipment remodel and an expanded interior drive-thru area. 4G Development filed it; signage is under a separate permit.
- **Pei Wei Asian Express** (BLD2026-08838, 50 S Main St, Suite FC103): A $75,000 renovation of a 1,133 sq ft food-court restaurant space in a downtown shopping mall.

**Planning pipeline**

Two minor land-division filings came in. One is a lot consolidation called "Jefferson Park Lot Consolidation" at 1084 S West Temple (PLNSUB2026-00915). The other is a lot line adjustment at 851 N 6880 W (26TMP-023845); it has no other details yet.

**Themes**

- **Sugar House retail:** Two filings sit side by side. Chick-fil-A's $1.37M remodel at 1206 E 2100 S is next to a $40,000 build-out for perfume retailer "bosco" at 1201 E Wilmington Ave.
- **Metech at 369 S Orange St:** Three trade records (plumbing, mechanical and a placeholder) relate to an office addition inside a warehouse. They are tied to earlier building permit BLD2026-06590.
- **West-side industrial area:** Several filings cluster here, including the Power HRG build-out, a $520,000 roof repair after a truck struck a building at 2590 W 1500 S, and an electrical job for National Vision.
- **Child care at Liberty Corner:** A $187,025 electrical permit points to a new A-Z Building Blocks child care center at 262 W 1300 S.
