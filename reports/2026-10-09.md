# New SLC commercial permits and planning applications: Sep 25 – Oct 09, 2026

**23 new record(s).**

**Highlights**

- **Ironhorse Spec Office Warehouse, 4750 W 2100 S** (BLD2026-08299, with BLD2026-08303 and BLD2026-08311): trade permits filed for new construction of a 101,239 sq ft speculative office/warehouse in Ninigret Park. The electrical portion alone is valued at $359,999. The main building permit is BLD2026-00787.

No other record this period clears the high bar. Worth a look at medium importance:

- **Church Office Building Level 4, 50 E North Temple** (BLD2026-08310): a 21,888 sq ft office remodel designed by ZGF Architects. No job value has been given yet.
- **Granary Lofts, 450 W 700 S** (BLD2026-08320): plumbing permit for the podium and fixtures, tied to building permit BLD2025-09869.
- **King's English Bookshop airport stores, 3920 W 900 N** (BLD2026-08309, BLD2026-08301): refresh of two co-branded concessions in the terminal.

**Themes**

- **Ironhorse cluster:** three trade permits (plumbing, electrical, HVAC) at 4750 W 2100 S point to the warehouse moving into construction.
- **Airport concessions:** NCA Partners of Dallas filed four small renovations of existing terminal retail stores. They are Trip Advisor/King's English, KSL/King's English, SLC Essentials and The Goods Express, totaling about $110k.
- **Proximity apartments, 780 N 900 W:** five permits add in-unit washer/dryer hookups, starting with a few test units.
- **Downtown office work:** small office remodels at Ray Quinney (36 S State) and 257 Tower.
- **Old Clock at Zions Bank, 102 S Main:** a small trench permit to bring new electrical service to the clock (a duplicate record was voided).
