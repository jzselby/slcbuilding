# New SLC commercial permits and planning applications: Oct 09 – Oct 23, 2026

**17 new record(s).**

**Highlights**

- **Salt Palace demolition begins (BLD2026-08736, BLD2026-08742), 100 S West Temple St.** Salt Lake County's Salt Palace Convention Center and Entertainment District project has filed for Phase 1 demolition. The work covers Exhibit Halls 2 and 3 and the north meeting-room stack, 184,107 sq ft in all. Each permit declares $3M. Demolition is slated to run Feb. 1 to Sept. 1, 2027. The rest of the convention center and Abravanel Hall stay open during this phase.
- **Electric Records seeks OK for live performances (PLNZAD2026-00908), 16 E Kensington Ave.** The Planning application asks for an administrative interpretation on accessory live performance at the business.
- **Toastique coming to downtown (BLD2026-08747, BLD2026-08748, BLD2026-08739), 245 S State St.** The gourmet toast, juice and coffee bar is fitting out a vacant 1,100 sq ft space.
- **9th Industrial buildings (BLD2026-08719, BLD2026-08720), 2750 W 900 S.** Fire-alarm permits were filed for two new core-and-shell industrial buildings of 94,050 and 78,375 sq ft.

**Planning pipeline**

One Planning application this period: an administrative interpretation (in prescreen) on whether Electric Records at 16 E Kensington Ave may host accessory live performances.

**Themes**

- Downtown dominates, led by the start of the county's Salt Palace redevelopment. Toastique's three trade permits on State Street are another downtown filing.
- The west-side industrial pipeline continues with the two 9th Industrial buildings.
- The remaining records are tenant-improvement trade permits and routine work, including:
  - Newmark Mountain sprinkler work on a 43,752 sq ft remodel
  - LifeStance Health electrical work on Brickyard Rd
  - Cleo Skin & Laser electrical work on Foothill Dr
