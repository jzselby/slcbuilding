# New SLC commercial permits and planning applications: Sep 16 – Sep 30, 2026

**148 new record(s).**

**Highlights**
- **BLD2026-07883**, 56 S Rio Grande St: Nuton lab and office interior remodel (30,765 sq ft of work area), $3.0M, filed by GSBS Architects.
- **BLD2026-07890**, 210 E 400 S: Salt Lake City Corporation renovation of the Old Main Library's three above-grade floors, $2.8M.
- **BLD2026-07729**, 650 S Main St: Fidelity interior alterations on floors 1 and 2 (9,265 sq ft), $2.63M.
- **BLD2026-07727 / 07730 / 07732 / 07728**, 848–954 S 4400 W: four Torus battery energy storage installations at $1.2M each ($4.8M total). A fifth, BLD2026-07723, was voided.
- **BLD2026-07869**, 350 W 800 S: Postino Granary restaurant interior upfit (5,656 sq ft), $1.1M.
- **PLNPCM2026-00805**, 3500 N 2200 W: partial rezone from M-1A to RMF-30 and RMF-45, with a development agreement.
- **BLD2026-07875**, 1116 S Richards St: HVAC sub-permit for Hive on 11th, a new 7-story, 169-unit affordable multifamily building (Bonneville Builders).

**Planning pipeline**
The 15 Planning records include three zoning amendments: the M-1A-to-multifamily rezone at 3500 N 2200 W, and two at 16 W 800 S, one of which proposes changing parking/automotive use to a parking garage. There are also six final plats, among them Crossroads Port Phase 1 (6780 W North Temple) and 1599 Major Street Townhomes, plus a Historic Landmark Commission new-construction application at 1880 E Yale Ave.
