# New SLC commercial permits and planning applications: Oct 07 – Oct 21, 2026

**19 new record(s).**

**Highlights**

- **BLD2026-08655**, 265 S State St: A $500,000 remodel would turn an existing two-story, 3,260 sq ft building into a restaurant. The project is called "Cramer House." Status: Need Info.
- **BLD2026-08645**, 777 S 200 E: New rough and finish plumbing for **Suan Wasatch Thai**, described as a small restaurant. It is tied to building permit BLD2026-01649.
- **BLD2026-08663**, 245 S State St, Unit A: A new CaptiveAire kitchen-hood fire suppression system for **Tony's Burritos Breakfast and Limes**.

**Themes**

- **Downtown restaurant cluster:** Three food-service filings sit within a block of each other on State Street and 200 East (245 S State, 265 S State, and Vessel Men's Health at 275 S 200 E). Suan Wasatch Thai is a little farther south.
- **Recognizable employers, mostly behind-the-scenes work:**
  - Amazon (SUT3, 620 S 5700 W, a 385,000 sq ft facility): a $100k emergency-responder radio system install.
  - National Vision (2525 W 1700 S): two linked permits, mechanical plus $40k electrical, to expand its AR room.
  - Lynk Engineers: sprinkler changes for a 35,791 sq ft tenant improvement on floors 2–3 of 150 E Social Hall Ave.
- **Northwest Quadrant and west side:**
  - Heaters for the "NWQ 4.1" shell building at 7744 W 700 N.
  - A $281k rooftop solar array for Granite Construction at 1059 N Warm Springs Rd.
  - Minor fire-alarm work at the airport (SLCIA).
- **Repeat filer:** Dance & Associates filed two matching $10k suite remodels (Suites 125 and 130) at 4750 W Wiley Post Way.
- **No Planning applications** were filed in this batch.
