# New SLC commercial permits and planning applications: Oct 10 – Oct 24, 2026

**8 new record(s).**

**Highlights**

- **Western Garden Apartments** (BLD2026-08763, 550 S 600 E): a fire-sprinkler permit for a new apartment building with parking and four levels of units, about 200,541 sq ft, in the Central City Historic District. It is tied to building permit BLD2024-09614.
- **Utah Power Credit Union** (PLNSUB2026-00911, 5567 W 220 S): a preliminary subdivision to carve out a lot on an undeveloped parcel for a new credit union branch building.
- **Oh No Bagels** (BLD2026-08777, 906 E 900 S): a $182,500 build-out converting a 1,217 sq ft former Great Clips into a bagel shop.
- **Pei Wei Asian Express** (26TMP-023685, 50 S Main St): renovation of a restaurant space in a shopping-mall food court.

**Planning pipeline**

Two subdivision filings came in:
- A preliminary plat for the Utah Power Credit Union branch on the west side.
- An IHC lot line adjustment at 709 S Main St, a property that carries a development agreement.

**Themes**

- **Restaurants:** Two food tenants are moving into existing spaces (Pei Wei downtown, Oh No Bagels at 9th and 9th area address 906 E 900 S).
- **Main Street corridor:** Activity clusters along Main Street at 50 S, 650 S and 709 S.
- **Routine work:** The remaining items are small interior and trade jobs: an iCapital office stair, a fire-alarm upgrade and a steel storage building.
