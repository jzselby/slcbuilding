# New SLC commercial permits and planning applications: Oct 16 – Oct 30, 2026

**24 new record(s).**

**Highlights**

- **Zoning amendment at 780 S Guardsman Way** (PLNPCM2026-00928): A Planning Commission zoning amendment is pending on a parcel in the Armory 4 Lot Minor subdivision. The record does not yet describe the proposal or name the applicant.
- **Beaumont Bakery & Cafe coming to Trolley Square** (BLD2026-08990, BLD2026-08988; 602 E 500 S, Suite D101): Electrical ($55,715) and mechanical sub-permits were filed for the cafe's build-out. Work includes a commercial kitchen hood and a rooftop make-up air unit. Both are tied to parent permit BLD2026-05173.
- **Chatime restaurant** (26TMP-024122; 385 S 400 E): Permit filed for the interior finish-out of an existing space into a Chatime restaurant.

**Planning pipeline**

One new Planning application this period: a zoning amendment near Guardsman Way (PLNPCM2026-00928). Details are not yet posted.

**Themes**

- **Restaurants:** Food and drink openings lead the period, with Beaumont Bakery & Cafe at Trolley Square and Chatime downtown.
- **Mid-size jobs worth a look:**
  - An 11,100 sq ft tenant improvement for A to Z Building Blocks in shell space at Liberty Corner Apartments (BLD2026-08989, with plumbing BLD2026-08959).
  - A $350,000 electrical renovation at Key Bank Tower (BLD2026-08994).
  - A $453,950 CT and X-ray remodel labeled PCH at 100 N Mario Capecchi Dr (BLD2026-08968).
  - A temporary Tesla Megacharger for electric semi trucks at 1038 S 300 W (BLD2026-08977).
- **Repeat filers:**
  - SkyWest's hangar paint booth at 1129 N 3950 W drew two fire-protection permits.
  - Secure Mechanical filed three identical permits for Suites 100, 200 and 300 at 369 S Orange St.
  - One fence contractor filed two linked permits for about 2,725 linear feet of 8-ft Trex fencing ($231,600 combined) along a south property line near 1300 E and I-80.
