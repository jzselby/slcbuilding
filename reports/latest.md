# New SLC commercial permits and planning applications: Sep 29 – Oct 13, 2026

**8 new record(s).**

**Highlights**

- **BLD2026-08389** – 1999 W 1700 S: $5.1M remodel to remove an existing printing press and install a new one (project "PCB Timson Replacement RotaJet") across a 16,390 sq ft work area. Filed by Dave Edwards of WPA Architecture.
- **BLD2026-08388** – 455 N 2200 W: Temporary-power electrical permit for a new University of Utah AirMed hangar on SLC International Airport property. The hangar will store a plane and a helicopter and is tied to building permit BLD2026-08371.
- **BLD2026-08392** – 40 N State St: $1.5M remodel of 1,930 sq ft of shelled first-floor space at Gateway Condos into a resident social amenity with kitchen/dining and exercise space.
- **PLNPCM2026-00872** – 1800 S 7200 W: New Planning Commission master plan amendment application, now in prescreen. No project details are posted yet.

**Planning pipeline**

A master plan amendment at 1800 S 7200 W is headed to the Planning Commission ($5,610 in fees paid). A separate zoning administration request seeks a nonconforming-use determination at 1448 E 900 S in the Douglas Park area. Neither record yet describes the proposal.

**Themes**

- WPA Architecture (Dave Edwards) filed both seven-figure permits this period: the $5.1M press replacement and the $1.5M Gateway Condos amenity.
- West-side industrial and airport-area work dominates: the AirMed hangar, a logistics dock expansion at 1150 S Gladiola St and warehouse sprinkler work at 8080 W 1400 N.
