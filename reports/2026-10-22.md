# New SLC commercial permits and planning applications: Oct 08 – Oct 22, 2026

**11 new record(s).**

**Highlights**

- **BLD2026-08679**, 100 S West Temple St: A $3M Phase 1 demolition permit for the Salt Palace Convention Center and Entertainment District county facilities. It would remove Exhibit Halls 2 and 3, the north meeting-room stack and the Utah Museum of Contemporary Art (UMOCA), about 224,107 sq ft across 2 buildings. Work is slated for Feb. 1 to Sept. 10, 2027, while the rest of the Salt Palace and Abravanel Hall stay open. Applicant: MHTN Architects.
- **PLNPCM2026-00905**, 660 N Redwood Rd: A Planning Commission zoning amendment seeks to turn an existing church house into a townhome rental development. The applicant is under contract to buy the property.

**Planning pipeline**

One new Planning Commission item this period: a zoning amendment at 660 N Redwood Rd that would replace a church house with townhome rentals. The unit count is not stated.

**Themes**

- **Downtown county project:** The Salt Palace and UMOCA demolition marks the start of the county's convention center and entertainment district redevelopment downtown.
- **Suspect valuation:** BLD2026-08693 (PACS Level 1 Tenant, Gateway Block B) lists a $300,000,000 job value. That is for a 7,094 sq ft remodel plus a 1,887 sq ft second-level floor addition, which suggests a data-entry error that is worth checking.
- **Routine work:** The rest of the batch is routine trade work. 369 S Orange St (Metech office build-out) has two related trade permits.
