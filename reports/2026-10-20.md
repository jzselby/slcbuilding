# New SLC commercial permits and planning applications: Oct 06 – Oct 20, 2026

**30 new record(s).**

**Highlights**

- **Foundry 43 demolition (BLD2026-08603, 537 W 600 S):** Application to demolish an existing office building in the MU-11 zone, with a declared job value of $200,000. The project name suggests redevelopment, but no replacement project is on file yet. Status: Need Info.
- **Magnolia Bakery (BLD2026-08602, 1400 S Foothill Dr, Suite 104):** Fire-alarm permit for the recognizable bakery brand. It references building permit BLD2-26-05850, which points to a build-out at this suite.

**Planning pipeline**

- PLNSUB2026-00899 (375 E Cleveland Ave) seeks final plat approval for a six-unit townhouse on vacant land in the Chicago Addition. PLNSUB2026-00894 (6400 W 300 S) is a final plat with no details yet.
- Nine more Planning records were opened the same day at 349 S 200 E, all on one parcel. They cover transit station development, an annexation, zoning incentives, landmark designation, economic hardship, reconstruction and a final plat. None has an applicant or project details, and one (PLNHLC2026-00892) lists "test" as its district and description, so they appear to be test entries. A quick check is warranted.

**Themes**

- **New food and retail tenants:** Several small food and retail build-outs were filed. They include Kyookie Bakery, a Korean bakery at 165 E 900 S ($151,505); Suan Wasatch Thai at 777 S 200 E, with a new kitchen hood; Space Tea at 1030 S State; Rio Acai at 550 S 300 W; and The Beauty Lounge at 1749 S State. The Kyookie site carries a notice that the strip mall is approved only for B occupancy, so a change of use there would trigger sprinklers.
- **West-side industrial:** Continental Battery is installing $260,000 of warehouse racking across 32,000 sq ft at 1770 S 5350 W.
- **Downtown:** Activity includes a roof replacement at the Little America Hotel and a $45,685 electrical permit for a Vessel Mens Health office at 275 S 200 E.
- **Routine work:** The rest is routine sprinkler, plumbing and electrical work.
