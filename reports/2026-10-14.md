# New SLC commercial permits and planning applications: Sep 30 – Oct 14, 2026

**27 new record(s).**

**Highlights**

- **BLD2026-08453**, 2025 S 900 W: An electric semi-truck charging station called "Megacharger" is proposed for an existing parking lot. It includes two pre-assembled Megacharger units, a switchboard, light poles and equipment pads, valued at $200,000.
- **PLNZAD2026-00875** and **26TMP-022926**, 451 S State St: Two zoning filings opened Oct. 14 on a parcel listed in the City & County Building subdivision. One asks for a determination of a nonconforming use and the other for an administrative interpretation.
- **26TMP-022800**, 2021 S Windsor St: A Planning Commission alley vacation petition, which would give up a public alley.
- **BLD2026-08439**, 3920 W 900 N (Salt Lake City airport): Fire-sprinkler work under the baggage conveyor for the airport's baggage handling system (BHS) expansion, covering 6,100 sq ft. A companion permit (BLD2026-08421) reworks sprinklers in an egress hallway.

**Planning pipeline**

Four Planning applications were filed:
- the two zoning filings at 451 S State St
- an alley vacation on Windsor Street
- a preliminary subdivision at 5567 W 220 S on the west side

None has a description or applicant listed yet.

**Themes**

- **Hightower, 40 S 900 E:** Contractor BCC filed several permits for a floor-by-floor remodel of residential units in this 15-story, 105,552 sq ft building. The work covers the 12th floor, the 14th floor, and the lower level and Floor 1, with eight units per floor.
- **Large west-side warehouses:** Smaller permits point to tenant activity in big buildings:
  - Torus Giga One, a 545,180 sq ft tenant build-out at 5552 W 700 S
  - a McLane security cage in a 306,265 sq ft building at 2611 W 2950 N
  - Seagull Diesel racking at 2217 N Redwood Rd
- **Paired trade permits:** Cleo Skin + Laser at 1400 S Foothill Dr filed both plumbing and mechanical permits. A suite at 46 W 300 S also filed both plumbing and electrical permits.
