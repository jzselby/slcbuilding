# New SLC commercial permits and planning applications: Sep 18 – Oct 02, 2026

**18 new record(s).**

**Highlights**

- **Holland & Hart law office remodel, $19M**: BLD2026-08108, 222 S Main St. Big D Construction filed to remodel 60,830 sq ft across levels 20-22 of the 22-story 222 South Main building. The work replaces all finishes, changes mechanical and electrical systems and adds connecting stairs between floors.
- **North Bench Pump Station replacement**: BLD2026-08109, 925 N Hilltop Rd. This is a Salt Lake City Public Utilities project to replace the culinary-water pump station. This record is a gas-line plumbing permit tied to building permit BLD2025-06988.
- **Uchi Japanese restaurant**: BLD2026-08129, 739 S 400 W, Suite A. A mechanical permit for the restaurant's tenant improvement, tied to building permit BLD2026-01087.
- **Wisteria and The Agency**: BLD2026-08135, 244 S Edison St. A $350K conversion of a 4,070 sq ft office building into a restaurant with a commercial kitchen and bar seating.
- **Rio Acai**: BLD2026-08133, 570 S 300 W, Suite 103. A $350K tenant improvement of a 1,571 sq ft space.
- **Chewy Vet Care**: BLD2026-08113, 1400 S Foothill Dr. A fire-alarm permit for a tenant improvement adding a Chewy Vet Care clinic.
- **UTA CNG facility**: BLD2026-08114, 669 W 200 S. Trench drains are being added under associated permit bld-2025-07327.

**Themes**

- **Restaurants:** Three new restaurant build-outs were filed in the same batch: Uchi, Wisteria and Rio Acai. Wisteria and Rio Acai are $350K each.
- **Public infrastructure and energy:** The city pump station, UTA's CNG site and a $200K EV megacharger installation at 2025 S 900 W all appear.
- **Follow-on trade permits:** Many records are add-ons to earlier building permits, including Medline's conveyor work (BLD2026-06018) and Silos Building 2's $290K fire alarm at 440 W Silo Park Ln. The larger projects behind them may already be underway.
- **No Planning records:** No Planning applications were filed in this batch.
