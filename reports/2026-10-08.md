# New SLC commercial permits and planning applications: Sep 24 – Oct 08, 2026

**18 new record(s).**

**Highlights**

- **BLD2026-08257**, 1059 N Warm Springs Rd: A **$2.88M** new solar project for Granite Construction, filed by ES Solar. The record was voided because it was filed under the wrong permit type, and the applicant was told to refile as a commercial solar permit. Watch for the new filing.
- **BLD2026-08274**, 2100 S near Main St and West Temple: Oak Hollow Electric plans to rebuild traffic signals at two 2100 South intersections for UDOT, valued at $300K. The record was voided as filed with the wrong department, and the work needs a right-of-way permit. Expect traffic control between the two intersections.

**Planning pipeline**

The only Planning item is a preliminary subdivision at 1436 S El Rey St (PLNSUB2026-00858). The owner wants to rebuild a single-family home there after the prior house was demolished.

**Themes**

- **West-side industrial and office build-outs:** Architect AE Urbia filed three office TIs near 1100 S and 5000 W:
  - two $300K Seefried Industrial spec offices in a 132,700 sq ft warehouse (BLD2026-08255, -08256);
  - a $347,545 Biovation office remodel (BLD2026-08267).
- **Two new consumer-facing spaces:** a $308K pool and spa at Lotus Alchemy, 48 W 700 S, and a new kitchen hood at Cafe Plov&Borsch, 55 N Redwood Rd.
- **Flats at Folsom:** a $300K fire-alarm system at 16 S 800 W. The record carries a development agreement requiring 2- and 3-bedroom units and active use along the Folsom Trail.
- Much of the rest is routine trade work or voided and test records.
